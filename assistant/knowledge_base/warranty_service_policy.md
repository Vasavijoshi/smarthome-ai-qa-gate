# Warranty and Service Policy

> Fictional product notice: This policy belongs to invented products written for the SmartHome AI QA Gate project. It does not describe any real company.

## Warranty periods

Every product has a standard warranty that starts on the date of purchase shown on the invoice. The Vayu F1 ceiling fan has a 2-year product warranty and a 5-year warranty on the BLDC motor. The Kavach L1 smart lock has a 1-year warranty. The Jal P2 water purifier has a 1-year warranty on the unit. The Mixo M3 mixer grinder has a 2-year product warranty and a 5-year warranty on the motor. A warranty is valid only in the country where the product was bought.

## What is covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and manufacturing that appear during normal home use. Within the warranty period, a faulty part is repaired or replaced free of charge, including the labour of the service technician. If a product cannot be repaired, it may be replaced with the same or an equivalent model. A replaced part or product is covered only for the rest of the original warranty period.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover consumable parts, such as purifier filters, the RO membrane, the UV lamp after its first year, remote control batteries, lock batteries or grinder gaskets. It does not cover damage caused by wrong installation, voltage fluctuation, water entry, dropping the product, misuse, or use for commercial purposes. It does not cover products that have been opened or repaired by anyone who is not an authorised technician, or products whose serial number has been removed. Cosmetic damage such as scratches and dents is not covered.

## How to raise a service request

Service requests are raised in the HomeMate app. Open the Support page, choose Raise a service request, select the product, describe the problem, and upload a photo of the invoice. You can also use the service web form at service.homemate.test with the same details. Each request receives a ticket number by message within a few minutes. Keep the ticket number for any follow-up. A technician visit is usually scheduled within 2 working days in cities and within 5 working days in other areas.

## Charges outside warranty

After the warranty ends, repairs are charged for parts and labour. The technician gives an estimate before starting work, and work starts only after you accept it. A visit charge applies if you decline the estimate. Spare parts are available for at least 5 years after a model stops being sold. An extended warranty plan can be bought for the fan, the grinder and the purifier within 1 year of the purchase date; it adds one more year of cover for the same items as the standard warranty.

## Installation and demonstration

Free installation is included for the Vayu F1 fan, the Kavach L1 lock and the Jal P2 purifier when the request is raised within 30 days of purchase. The Mixo M3 grinder does not need installation, but a free demonstration visit can be requested. The customer must provide the fan hook or ceiling box, the door preparation for the lock, and a water inlet point and power socket for the purifier. Installation by an authorised technician is required to keep the lock and purifier warranties valid.

## Documents needed

Keep the original purchase invoice and the product serial number. The serial number is printed on a label on the product: on the motor housing for the fan, under the battery cover for the lock, on the back panel for the purifier and under the base for the grinder. A warranty claim without an invoice can be refused.

## Privacy of service records

Service records contain customer names and contact details. These records are used only to deliver service and are not shared with other customers. The HomeMate assistant does not give out any customer's contact details or service history.
