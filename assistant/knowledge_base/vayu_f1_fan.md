# Vayu F1 Ceiling Fan: User Manual

> Fictional product notice: Vayu F1 is an invented product written for the SmartHome AI QA Gate project. It does not describe any real fan.

## Overview

The Vayu F1 is a 1200 mm, three-blade ceiling fan with a brushless DC (BLDC) motor. It can be controlled with the supplied infrared remote or through the HomeMate app. The fan uses about 32 watts at its highest speed and about 6 watts at speed 1. The fan is meant for indoor rooms with a ceiling height of at least 2.6 metres. It must not be installed in bathrooms or open verandas, because the motor is not protected against water spray.

## Speed settings

The Vayu F1 has six speed levels, numbered 0 to 5. Speed 0 means the fan is off. Speed 1 is the slowest running speed and speed 5 is the fastest. Each step up adds roughly 60 revolutions per minute, from about 90 RPM at speed 1 to about 330 RPM at speed 5. Speed 3 is the recommended everyday speed for a medium bedroom. Pressing the plus button on the remote raises the speed by one level, and the minus button lowers it by one level. The fan remembers the last speed when power returns after a power cut.

## Timer

The timer switches the fan off after a set time. The timer can be set in steps of 30 minutes, and the longest timer is 480 minutes (8 hours). Setting the timer to 0 cancels it. When the timer ends, the fan goes to speed 0 and stays off until it is switched on again. Changing the speed does not cancel a running timer. The remaining timer time is shown in the HomeMate app.

## Modes

The fan has three modes. Normal mode keeps the chosen speed constant. Breeze mode varies the speed up and down around the chosen level every few seconds to feel like natural wind. Sleep mode is for the night: it lowers the speed by one level every hour until it reaches speed 1, and then it holds speed 1. Sleep mode works together with the timer. A mode is selected with the M button on the remote. The fan always starts in Normal mode after the power is switched off at the wall.

## Remote control

The remote uses one CR2032 coin cell. It works up to about 8 metres from the fan and needs a clear line of sight to the receiver in the motor housing. The remote has buttons for power, speed plus, speed minus, timer, mode and a light-off button that is not used on this model. To pair a new remote, switch the fan off at the wall, switch it back on, and within 30 seconds hold the power button on the remote for 5 seconds until the fan beeps twice.

## Cleaning

Always set the fan to speed 0 and switch it off at the wall before cleaning. Wipe the blades with a soft, damp cloth and then dry them with a dry cloth. Do not use abrasive powders, solvents or a dripping wet cloth, and never spray water or any liquid on the motor housing. Clean the blades about once a month in dusty rooms. Do not bend the blades while cleaning, because a bent blade causes wobbling.

## Troubleshooting

If the fan does not start, first check that the wall switch is on and that the speed is not set to 0. If the remote does not work, replace the CR2032 cell and make sure nothing blocks the line of sight to the motor housing. If the remote still does not respond, pair it again using the steps in the remote control section. If the fan wobbles, switch it off and check that all blade screws are tight and that no blade is bent; a small wobble at speed 5 is normal. If the fan makes a humming sound, check that it is connected to a normal wall switch and not to a dimmer switch, because a dimmer can damage the motor electronics. If the fan stops by itself, check whether a timer was set. If the fan beeps three times and stops, the motor has detected an overload, for example from a blocked blade; switch it off at the wall, remove the obstruction, wait one minute and switch it on again. If the fan runs at a lower speed than expected, check whether Sleep mode is on, because Sleep mode lowers the speed every hour; press the M button until Normal mode is selected. If the speed changes up and down by itself, Breeze mode is probably on. If the fan does not appear in the HomeMate app, make sure the fan was switched on at the wall during app setup and that the home hub is within range. If the fan switches on by itself after a power cut, this is expected, because the fan remembers its last speed; set the speed to 0 before switching off at the wall if you do not want this. If any of these problems continue, raise a service request as described in the warranty and service policy.

## Safety

Installation must be done by a qualified electrician. Do not try to open the motor housing, change the wiring or replace the regulator circuit yourself. Keep children away from the remote buttons, and never hang anything from the fan or the down rod.
