# Kavach L1 Smart Lock: User Manual

> Fictional product notice: Kavach L1 is an invented product written for the SmartHome AI QA Gate project. It does not describe any real lock.

## Overview

The Kavach L1 is a smart deadbolt lock for wooden main doors between 35 mm and 60 mm thick. It has an outdoor keypad unit and an indoor unit that holds the batteries and the thumb-turn. The lock can be opened with a PIN code, with the HomeMate app, or with the mechanical backup key. The account that first sets up the lock in the HomeMate app becomes the owner account. Only the owner can add or remove PIN codes and change lock settings.

## Locking and unlocking

To unlock from outside, wake the keypad by touching it, enter your PIN and press the tick key. To lock from outside, press and hold the lock key for 1 second. From inside, turn the thumb-turn. In the HomeMate app, locking happens straight away, but unlocking always asks for a confirmation first, so that the door is not opened by mistake. If a wrong PIN is entered 5 times in a row, the keypad is disabled for 3 minutes and the lock beeps continuously for 10 seconds. The mechanical key always works, even when the batteries are empty.

## PIN codes

A PIN must have 6 to 8 digits. The lock can store up to 20 PIN codes. A PIN can be given a time window, for example for a cook or a cleaner who should only enter between 8 am and 11 am. To hide your PIN from people watching, you may type extra random digits before or after the real PIN, up to 16 digits in total; the lock still opens if the real PIN is inside the digits typed.

## PIN reset by the owner

If the owner forgets their own PIN, the owner can reset it in the HomeMate app: open the lock page, go to Settings, choose Reset PIN, enter the owner account password, and then type a new PIN twice. The new PIN works immediately. If the owner has also lost access to the owner account, the lock can be returned to factory settings: open the battery cover on the indoor unit, press and hold the reset button for 10 seconds until a long beep sounds, and then set up the lock again in the app. A factory reset deletes all PIN codes and all users. The reset button is only reachable from inside the house, so a factory reset cannot be done from outside the door.

## Battery

The indoor unit uses 4 AA alkaline batteries. Typical battery life is about 12 months with around 10 unlocks a day. When the battery level falls below 20 percent, the keypad flashes red after each use and the HomeMate app shows a low-battery warning. Replace all four batteries at the same time and do not mix old and new batteries. If the batteries are completely empty and you are outside, connect a USB-C power bank to the emergency port under the keypad. This gives the lock enough power to accept a PIN; it does not charge the batteries.

## Auto-lock

Auto-lock relocks the door a set time after it is unlocked. Auto-lock is on by default with a delay of 30 seconds. The owner can change the delay from 10 to 180 seconds, or switch auto-lock off, in the lock settings in the HomeMate app. Auto-lock only works when the door is closed, because a magnetic door sensor checks that the door is shut before the bolt moves.

## Troubleshooting

If the keypad does not light up, replace the batteries or use the emergency USB-C port. If the bolt does not move fully, check that the door is aligned with the frame and that the strike hole is not blocked; a misaligned door makes the motor work harder and drains the batteries. If the app shows the lock as offline, move your phone or the home hub closer, because the lock communicates over a short-range radio link. If the lock beeps continuously, a wrong PIN was entered too many times; wait 3 minutes. If the thumb-turn feels stiff, check that the indoor unit screws are not over-tightened. If auto-lock does not relock the door, check that the door is fully closed and that the magnetic door sensor and its magnet are aligned; a gap of more than 10 mm stops auto-lock from working. If a guest PIN does not work, check its time window in the app, because a PIN with a time window is rejected outside those hours. If the keypad accepts the PIN but the bolt does not move, the batteries may be too weak to drive the motor even though the keypad lights up; replace them. If the lock makes a grinding noise, stop using the app to unlock and use the mechanical key until a technician has checked the motor. If the lock does not respond to the app after a battery change, wait one minute for it to reconnect to the home hub. For any fault that continues, raise a service request as described in the warranty and service policy.

## Safety and security

Never share the owner account password. Change PIN codes given to guests or workers when they no longer need access. Do not try to remove the lock body or open the indoor unit beyond the battery cover; this voids the warranty and can damage the anti-tamper sensor.
