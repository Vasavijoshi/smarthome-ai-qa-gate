# Mixo M3 Mixer Grinder: User Manual

> Fictional product notice: Mixo M3 is an invented product written for the SmartHome AI QA Gate project. It does not describe any real mixer grinder.

## Overview

The Mixo M3 is a 750-watt mixer grinder for home kitchens. It comes with three stainless steel jars and works on 230 volt household power. The Mixo M3 is not a connected device: it cannot be controlled from the HomeMate app, and HomeMate can only answer questions about it.

## Jars

The large 1.5-litre liquidizing jar is for juices, shakes and wet batters. The medium 1.0-litre dry jar is for grinding spices, rice and pulses. The small 0.4-litre chutney jar is for chutneys and small amounts of wet grinding. Never fill a jar beyond two thirds of its capacity, and never put hot liquids above 60 degrees Celsius in any jar, because the steam pressure can push the lid off. Make sure the lid is closed and locked before starting; the jar must sit fully on the base, otherwise the safety switch stops the motor from starting.

## Speed knob

The speed knob has five positions: P (pulse), 0 (off), 1 (low), 2 (medium) and 3 (high). Turn the knob to P and hold it for short bursts; it springs back to 0 when released. Use speed 1 for mixing and whipping, speed 2 for wet grinding and speed 3 for dry grinding of hard spices. Start at a low speed and increase it slowly. Do not run the grinder for more than 3 minutes at a time. After 3 minutes of use, switch it off and let the motor rest for at least 2 minutes before the next use.

## Overload protection

The motor has an overload protector that switches it off if the motor gets too hot or the jar is overloaded. If this happens, the grinder stops suddenly. Turn the knob to 0, unplug the grinder, and remove some of the contents from the jar. Wait about 10 minutes for the motor to cool down. Then press the red overload reset button under the base and start again at a low speed. If the overload protector trips again and again with a small load, raise a service request.

## Cleaning

Always unplug the grinder before cleaning. Wash the jars with warm water and mild dish soap right after use, and dry them upside down. The blades are very sharp, so clean them with a brush and not with your fingers. Wipe the motor base with a damp cloth only; never put the base in water or under a running tap. The jars are not dishwasher safe, because the dishwasher can damage the jar couplers and the rubber seals.

## Troubleshooting

If the grinder does not start, check that the jar is placed correctly and the lid is locked, and that the overload reset button has not tripped. If the jar leaks from the bottom, check that the rubber gasket under the blade assembly is seated correctly and is not cracked; spare gaskets are available from service. If the grinder makes a loud rattling sound, switch it off at once and check that the jar coupler is not worn. If there is a burning smell during the first few uses, this is normal for a new motor and should stop after about five uses; if it continues later, stop using the grinder and raise a service request. If the grinding is not fine enough, grind in smaller batches and use speed 3 for dry spices. If the motor runs but the blades do not turn, the coupler on the jar or on the motor base may be worn and must be replaced by service. If the motor slows down a lot during grinding, the jar is probably too full; stop, remove part of the contents and continue in two batches. If the lid becomes loose while grinding, stop the grinder and check that the lid gasket is fitted in its groove. If the jar is hard to remove from the base after use, wait until the motor has fully stopped and turn the jar gently instead of pulling it. If the blades become blunt after long use, the complete blade assembly can be replaced by service; the blades cannot be sharpened at home. Do not use the grinder if its power cord is damaged.

## Safety

Keep the grinder out of the reach of children. Never put your hand, a spoon or any object into the jar while the motor is running. Do not use the grinder outdoors. Do not try to rewire the plug or change the motor brushes yourself.
