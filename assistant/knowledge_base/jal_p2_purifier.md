# Jal P2 Water Purifier: User Manual

> Fictional product notice: Jal P2 is an invented product written for the SmartHome AI QA Gate project. It does not describe any real purifier.

## Overview

The Jal P2 is a wall-mounted water purifier for kitchens. It uses four stages: a sediment filter, a carbon filter, a reverse osmosis (RO) membrane and an ultraviolet (UV) lamp. It has a 7-litre storage tank and purifies about 15 litres per hour. The purifier is suitable for input water with total dissolved solids (TDS) up to 2000 mg per litre. The status and the remaining filter life can be seen on the front display and in the HomeMate app, but the purifier cannot be switched on or off from the app.

## Filter life

The display shows filter life as a percentage from 100 down to 0. The percentage is calculated from the volume of water purified and the time since the last filter change, whichever runs out first. When filter life falls to 10 percent, the display shows a yellow filter icon. At 0 percent the display shows error E4 and the purifier keeps working, but the water quality is no longer guaranteed. The sediment and carbon filters should be replaced every 6 months, and the RO membrane every 24 months, or earlier if the display asks for it. After a filter change, the filter life counter must be reset by a service technician or through the Filter Reset option in the HomeMate app.

## Flushing

Flushing removes loose carbon dust and air after a new installation or a filter change. To flush, let the purifier fill the tank completely and then drain all of the water through the tap. Repeat this two times, so that the first two full tanks of water are thrown away. Do not drink the water from these two tanks. A flush is also recommended if the purifier has not been used for more than 7 days.

## Error codes

E1 means the input water pressure is too low; check that the inlet valve is fully open. E2 means the tank level sensor has a fault; switch the purifier off and on again, and raise a service request if E2 returns. E3 means the UV lamp has failed; the purifier stops dispensing water until the lamp is replaced by a technician. E4 means the filter life is at 0 percent; replace the filters. E5 means a leak has been detected inside the unit; the purifier closes the inlet valve automatically. When you see E5, switch the purifier off at the wall, close the inlet tap and do not use it until a technician has checked it.

## Maintenance

Clean the outside of the purifier with a soft dry cloth. Clean the storage tank every 3 months: switch the purifier off, drain the tank, wipe the inside with a clean damp cloth and do not use soap or chemicals. Do not place the purifier in direct sunlight, because sunlight encourages algae in the tank. The waste water from the RO stage can be collected and used for cleaning floors or watering plants, but it is not suitable for drinking.

## Troubleshooting

If no water comes out, check that the display does not show E3 and that the tank is not empty after heavy use; the tank refills in about 30 minutes. If the water tastes different after a filter change, flush the purifier as described in the flushing section. If water flow is slow, the sediment filter may be blocked; check the filter life and replace the filter if needed. If the purifier makes a continuous humming sound when the tank is full, the auto shut-off may have failed; switch it off and raise a service request. If the display is blank, check the power socket and the adapter. If the purifier shows E1 often, the building water pressure may be too low for the purifier, and a service technician can advise whether a booster pump is needed. If the tap drips after you close it, the tap washer may be worn; a technician can replace it. If water leaks from under the unit without an E5 error, check the inlet and outlet pipe joints and tighten them gently by hand. If the filter life in the HomeMate app does not match the front display, refresh the app; the display is always the correct value. If the purifier shows E4 straight after a filter change, the filter life counter was not reset; use the Filter Reset option in the app or ask the technician. If the purifier starts and stops many times a minute, switch it off and raise a service request. Do not open the purifier body yourself; the internal parts carry mains voltage.

## Safety

The purifier must be installed by an authorised technician. Use it only with cold drinking-water supply lines. Keep the power adapter away from water and do not use the purifier if the power cord is damaged.
